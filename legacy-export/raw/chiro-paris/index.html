Choose Our Chiropractors

Dr. Gregory Thompson, Dr. Sean Welborn and Dr. Collins at Chiropractic Associates have provided complete chiropractic care to Northeastern Texas and Southeastern Oklahoma residents around Paris since 1998 and now we have a second location in Sulphur Springs, TX,

In addition to exceptional chiropractic care, our family-owned practice offers spinal decompression therapy, therapeutic therapy and rehab therapeutic exercises such as attended stretching with our rehab coaches. Utilizing modern treatments and examination techniques, we work towards providing effective relief for numerous conditions in as few visits as possible.

Unless you are suffering from chronic disease or pain, it is unlikely that our doctors will recommend long-term treatment or prepayment plans. We aim to provide cost-effective and efficient chiropractic care that includes pain relief for the symptoms you are experiencing. Offering treatment for some of the following chiropractic care that includes:

- Back and neck pain
- Knee pain
- Sciatica
- Herniated discs
- Pinched nerves
- Auto injuries

To maximize the benefits, we offer a combination of some of the following treatments:

- Chiropractic adjustments and manipulations
- Therapeutic massage, including lymphatic massage, and trigger point therapy.
- Electric muscle stimulation therapy
- Moist heat and cryotherapy
- Intersegmental traction and spinal decompression therapy
- Acupuncture
- Pediatric care

Due to our comprehensive range of services and the care we provide in our community, we have been voted “Best Chiropractic Center” by The Paris News, readers' choice award every year since 2005 and “Best Place for Massage” since 2008.
