Say Good-bye to Everyday Stress

At The Rub Club, we specialize in a variety of professional massage therapy treatments. From prenatal treatments to prepare you for giving birth to recovery from sports injuries, you can trust our therapists and chiropractors.

We'll work with you to find the best massage therapy to suit your needs. You just need to visit us on a regular basis or get a customized, regular program to address any individual health problem. Give us a call today at 903-739-9959!

For 15 years, The Rub Club has worked with pain management doctors to help ease patients' ailments. We are a family owned and operated massage parlor that specializes in medically sound massage therapy. The Rub Club proudly serves Paris, Texas, both as a business and as a member of the Paris Chamber Of Commerce. The Rub Club gladly welcomes community members of all ages.

When To Get a Massage

Our massage therapists specialize in pain management. Have you been in an accident or had an operation done recently? We can help. When you're injured, your body often uses your muscles in odd ways to compensate. Massage therapy relieves these sore muscles. Are you pregnant? Our therapists are trained in prenatal massage, easing your discomfort while preparing your body to give birth. If you're an athlete, our massage therapists can help you get back on the field. The Rub Club is trained to help athletes recover and rejuvenate after a sports injury so they can heal quickly and correctly.

Treatments We Offer

The Rub Club knows that different problems require different solutions. That's why we are proud to offer a variety of treatments, including

- Sports massage
- Prenatal massage
- Deep-tissue massage
