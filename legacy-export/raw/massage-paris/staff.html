Meet Our Massage Therapists

  • Brandi Boren
    LMT/Manager

    My name is Brandi. I have been married since February of 2005 and  have one child born in 2014. I was born and raised in Paris, TX and graduated from Paris High School in 2003. I received my massage training from Bodywork Study School of Massage, completing the program in 2005. I have worked as a massage therapist for The Rub Club since 2005. I have worked in the health field since 2004, as a therapy assistant for chiropractic doctors at Chiropractic Associates. I have undergone further training in deep tissue massage, trigger point therapy, myofascial release, and the treatment of fibromyalgia. I especially enjoy providing prenatal massages and have a special interest in treating clients with medical conditions such as tension-type headaches and neck or lower back pain. I enjoy being outdoors, hunting and fishing, and being a mom.

  • Channety Wooten

    My name is Channety. I was raised in Paris, TX and graduated from Paris High School in 2005. My massage training was completed in 2009 at the Bodywork Study School of Massage. I also earned my associate degree in Radiology from Paris Junior College in 2016. I have been a therapist at The Rub Club since 2011. I specialize in Swedish massage, deep tissue and sports massage. I enjoy helping our patients and love to see their results of being pain free!

  • Rosylin Wilmore

    My name is Rosylin. I was born in Clarksville, TX and grew up in Tulia, TX. I graduated from Tulia High School and moved to San Diego, CA. I lived in California for over 32 years and recently moved back to Clarksville. While in San Diego, I attended Concorde Career Institute where I received my certification as a Massage Therapist. I have been a massage therapist for over 12 years and truly enjoy relieving people of headaches and pains. My specialty is Swedish massage, trigger point therapy and stretching. My favorite thing to do is spending time with my grandchildren.

  • Shely Cox

    My name is Shely Cox. I have worked as a massage therapist for the Rub Club since summer of 2022. I graduated in 2020 from Body Business school in Durant, Oklahoma. I'm also a dedicated 5th grade teacher in Antlers, Oklahoma. I specialize in Swedish massage. I find it very rewarding to help others feel better.

  • Ana Vasquez

    My name is Ana Vasquez. I was raised in Los Angeles where I attended California Arts College in 2001. I moved to Texas and attended School of Massage. I'm committed to promoting my client's well-being and enjoy being able to help them relax by relieving their pain, tension and reduce stress.

  • Baileigh Hervon

    My name is Baileigh. I’m from Hugo, OK and graduated from North Lamar High School in 2018. My favorite things to do are spend time with my family, cooking, and reading. In 2023, I completed my certification as a Massage Therapist from Body Business School of Massage Therapy. I genuinely enjoy relieving people of aches and pains. My specialty is Swedish massage, trigger point therapy and hot stone massage.
